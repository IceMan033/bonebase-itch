Bonebase 1.0 is out! Quick recap for anyone who just landed here: I can draw a character once, but every time I tried to animate one, its arms changed length every frame and by frame 8 it was a different person. So I built a tool that does the part I'm bad at. You make a skeleton, give it a rough body, animate it with live IK, clips and a dope sheet, and export it to Aseprite as layers to draw over.

The plan is to ship something every week, mostly so I have an excuse to keep working on it. Here's the order, nearest first. Only the first one has a date, because I've met me.

Next week: reference images (1.1)

Load a drawing, a photo or another sprite under the rig, and build or pose the skeleton on top of it. Basically tracing, but the skeleton does the homework.

  • From a file, from the clipboard, or from any sprite you have open, at any of its frames.
  • One reference for the whole clip, with keys for position, scale, rotation, opacity and flip. For when your sketch is never where the rig is (mine never is).
  • Or a different picture on single frames: put your key pose drawings on their frames and match the rig to each one.
  • Drawn under the skin, or over it to trace. Saved in the sprite with the rig, never exported.

After that: an animation library

Yes, I know. I told you I can't animate. That's the whole point: this sprint is 10 frames with IK on the legs and arms, and Bonebase did most of the thinking. I mostly dragged feet around and argued with the knees.

Same guy, walking this time, because he deserved a break. The library will be ready-made cycles like these two that you drop on your own rig and then tweak, so nobody has to start a walk cycle from a blank canvas again. Including me.

Planned: your own rig library

Save your rigs, each with its animations, and open them in any sprite. Export them and carry them between projects, instead of rebuilding the same skeleton every time you start a new character (which I have done more times than I'll admit).

Planned: rigs from a silhouette

Start from the outline of a drawing and get a rig that fits it. This one is still an idea living in my notes. I'll write about it when I know how it should work, and not one devlog before.

If one of these matters more to you than the rest, or something you need is missing, tell me in the comments. The order can change, and honestly your ideas are probably better than my knees.
